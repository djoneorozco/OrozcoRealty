OrozcoRealty is website service build to provide financial and psychological awareness to the military community.  Designed as a 'Virtual Realtor' our service provides un-biased and comprehensive data analytics through the use of "Duke's ChatGPT API". Users benefit by getting clear and insightful data through the use our Platforms and individualized dashboards. OrozcoRealty will then sell leads to realtors for a percentage fee.

VISION STATEMENT: To become the military community’s most trusted source of real-estate intelligence by transforming home-buying uncertainty into clarity through a unified A.I.-driven readiness systemy.
‍
MISSION STATEMENT: Our mission is to deliver precise real-estate insight to the military community through an A.I.-powered system that blends financial analytics, behavioral psychology, and intuitive visualization.We equip every service member with a personalized readiness profile—transforming complex data into actionable guidance that strengthens financial resilience, emotional preparedness, and long-term homeownership outcomes.
Target Market & Hook: Veterans, Active Duty and their family members. Provide a simple all in one Permanent Change of Station (PCS) tool that captures next duty station economical data and insight using User's Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH) and Base Pay.
