STAGE 02 / UNDERSTAND YOUR BUDGET

Close ×

Would buying comfortably fit our life?

See the money remaining each month after the costs and savings your household plans to cover.

Explore the monthly budget tool ↗ Separate tool: VA payment & cash-to-close example ↗

The budget tool uses totals you enter. Payment and upfront-cash examples are separate; they do not decide affordability or approve a loan.

  • Count principal and interest, property taxes, insurance, applicable HOA costs, utilities, maintenance, and repair reserves.
  • Include household spending, debts, savings goals, and costs that vary. Keep upfront cash separate from monthly costs.
  • Compare BAH with estimated housing expenses. Include it only once in take-home income; count escrowed taxes and insurance only once.

Money remaining each month

Take-home income − total housing − everyday spending − debt payments − planned savings − other reserves.

Keep each cost in one category. Adjust the assumptions to explore a different month. This household budget is separate from VA residual-income underwriting.

STAGE 03 / YOUR HOMEBUYING GUIDE

Close ×

We want to buy. What happens next?

Prepare for the decisions between a first lender conversation and your first day at home.

Explore the VA Command Center ↗ CFPB guide: loan choices through closing ↗
  • Learn about VA loans and other financing options, the Certificate of Eligibility, and preapproval preparation.
  • Investigate funding fees, closing costs, possible exemptions, and state or local tax benefits and assistance. Eligibility and availability vary.
  • Learn how to choose and work with a real estate professional, then prepare for the search, offers, inspections, appraisal, closing, and ownership.

The VA Command Center covers VA basics, cost examples, and lender preparation. The official CFPB guide adds broader homebuying education. Local benefits and a complete PCSUnited purchase-process guide are not yet integrated.

Enter at any stage. Revisit what matters. Base housing, renting, buying, and waiting are all valid choices.
