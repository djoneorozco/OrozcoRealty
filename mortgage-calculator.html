About The Orozco Realty Financial Health Snapshot

This quick snapshot helps buyers pressure-test a home before getting emotionally locked in. Enter your income, monthly expenses, and home scenario to see whether the estimated payment fits your financial picture.

The core output is simple: total monthly income minus monthly expenses minus estimated mortgage payment equals residual income.
